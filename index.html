HOW TO PLAY

🎯 AIM

Touch and drag near your tank to aim its cannon.

🚙 MOVE

Hold the movement buttons. You have 3 seconds of movement per turn.

💥 SHOOT

Choose your shell power and tap FIRE. Gravity curves the shell's path.

❤️ DAMAGE

Explosions damage nearby tanks. Damage decreases with distance.

🏆 WIN

Reduce your opponent's health to zero to win.
