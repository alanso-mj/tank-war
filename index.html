FIELD MANUAL

HOW TO PLAY

01

MOVEMENT

Use the left and right controls to move your tank. Each turn allows up to three seconds of movement.

02

AIM YOUR CANNON

Drag across the battlefield to adjust your cannon. Use the dotted trajectory preview to estimate your shot.

03

CONTROL FIRING POWER

Adjust the power slider to change the shell's speed and effective range.

04

DESTROY THE ENEMY

Explosions damage nearby tanks. Stay alert: your own tank can also take explosion damage.

05

TAKE YOUR TURN

Firing ends your turn. Reduce the opposing tank's health to zero to win the match.
