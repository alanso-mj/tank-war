HOW TO PLAY

🕹️

MOVE YOUR TANK

Use the left and right buttons. Movement is limited to 3 seconds per turn.

🎯

AIM YOUR CANNON

Drag on the battlefield to adjust your cannon. Watch the dotted trajectory preview.

💥

SET POWER AND FIRE

Adjust the power slider and tap FIRE. Your shot must finish before the next turn begins.

❤️

DESTROY THE OPPONENT

Each tank starts with 100 HP. Explosion damage decreases with distance from the blast centre.

🏆

WIN THE BATTLE

Reduce the opponent's HP to zero to win.
